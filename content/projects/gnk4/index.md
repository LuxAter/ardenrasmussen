Specula v2 is still a work in progress, and is a ground up re-implementation of
Specula. Following along with the work from [Physically Based Rendering: From
Theory to Implementation](https://pbrt.org/) (PBRT). Specula is being expanded
to support GPU acceleration for the rendinger, along with greater support for a
more flexible BxDF material system.
